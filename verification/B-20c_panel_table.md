# B-20c: reference audio in the window

Generated by `app/src/main.rs`, module `editing`. Re-run with `cargo test --workspace`.

D-71 decided what an audio layer is and B-20b built it in the core, checked in `verification/B-20b_audio_table.md`. This is the window's half: sound files in the import and relink dialogs, a layer made from a sound is an audio layer, the level is `layer.set_gain`, the speaker is the switch every layer has, and the page fetches a sound file's bytes from `/sound` to play it with its own decoder.

Every row calls what the window calls, on `Fixtures/projects/cel_holds_project.json` and the WAV files of `Fixtures/audio/media/`, and reads back what the page is given.

## What this does not cover

What is heard, whether it stays with the picture, and what the bars look like. Sound and sync are judged by ear: that is `verification/B-20c_audio_playtest.md`, for a person.
