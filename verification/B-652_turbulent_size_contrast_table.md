# B-652: Turbulent Noise's cell and contrast match After Effects' (D-772)

Seven tutorials from seven creators read our Turbulent Noise 1.36 to 1.51 times too coarse and its greys 0.49 to 0.69 as spread in After Effects. Turbulent Noise now works its noise at c = size / 1.5, Fractal Noise's cell (D-756), so Size 100 at Scale 100 is a 66.7-pixel cell, and its grey is 0.5 + 0.5 n 0.64 contrast / 100 + brightness / 100. The cell widths are counted on a 400-pixel-wide Block noise of one octave; the spreads on a smooth noise of 6 octaves; the fixtures' numbers are the reference's, regenerated by D-772.
