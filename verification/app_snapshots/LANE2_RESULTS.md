# Lane 2 effect check: Distort, Perspective, Blur & Sharpen, Transition, Time

Every built effect in these five sections of `docs/effects/EFFECTS.md`, drawn in the real app by `tools/app_snapshot.py` (frames 0, 12, 24) on the curl_noise test scene: a painted mountain background (layer 1), a soft red circle moving on 1s, a hard-edged yellow/cyan layer on 2s, a 50% green square on 3s. 1920x1080, 24 fps. Each row: the processor (cpu) picture and the graphics-card (card) picture, compared over the full frame. Times are the tool's shot times for frame 12 (one run, not a timing test). Machine: NVIDIA GeForce RTX 4070 Ti SUPER, the release build at target/release/anime_compositor_app.exe as of 2026-10-13.

Effects checked: 92 (plus 8 extra checks to isolate problems). Not built: 2.

- **works**: 70
- **looks wrong**: 0
- **cpu/card differ**: 21
- **fails**: 1
- **not testable here**: 0
- **not built**: 2

## Bugs found (most serious first)

1. **3D Glasses ignores named view layers.** With `left_view` or `right_view` naming a layer (its own id, or another layer, hidden or shown) the picture is exactly the same as with no effect (0 pixels changed, three tries: `three_d_glasses`, `three_d_glasses_shown`, `three_d_glasses_right`). With both views "" (the layer itself) it works (`three_d_glasses_self`). No message anywhere.
2. **A setting out of range or a wrong word makes an effect vanish silently.** Camera Lens Blur with highlight threshold 200 (its range is 0-100) and Glow Wipe (CC Light Wipe) with shape "round" (its words are doors, radial, rectangular) are both drawn exactly as if the effect were off; the shot record's `report` is empty and there is no error. The engine has messages for these (src/effects.rs) but they do not reach the app picture or the snapshot record. Document 28 asks for an explicit diagnosis. Re-run with valid settings, both work.
3. **CC Blobbylize: cpu and card pictures differ across the picture** (max 198 levels, average about 0.7, on every frame). Not a few edge pixels: a real mismatch.
4. **Radial Blur (spin): cpu and card differ in a small patch** (max R50 G111 B5, average about 0.04), probably round the centre point.
5. **Warps with a transparent outline differ by a handful of pixels** between cpu and card (max 16 to 255, average under 0.01): CC Bend It, CC Bender, CC Flo Motion, CC Griddler, CC Lens, CC Page Turn, CC Power Pin, CC Slant, CC Split, CC Split 2, Corner Pin, Displacement Map, Optics Compensation, Polar Coordinates, Ripple, Transform. Likely where the warped picture's edge is sampled; location not checked with a full-size shot.
6. **CC Cylinder and CC Sphere: small widespread cpu/card difference** (max 11, average about 0.03) over the lit surface, likely lighting rounding.
7. **Gaussian Blur: max cpu/card difference 3** (limit 2), average 0.016. Marginal.

Settings notes (not bugs): Twirl's radius is in pixels (40 barely shows; 400 used). Liquify, CC Vector Blur, Drop Shadow, Bulge and CC Bender are faint at the settings used.
