## The transparency grid

| Where | Before | After |
|---|---|---|
| The command finder (Ctrl+Shift+P), "Transparency grid" | said G and pressed G, which picks the pen | says Alt+G and presses Alt+G, which shows or hides the grid |
| The hint line at the bottom of the window | "D, G, Alt+A" | "D, Alt+G, Alt+A" |

The grid moved from G to Alt+G in B-24c, when G went to the pen as After Effects has it. The
key itself was moved then; these two places that name it were not.

To check it yourself: press Ctrl+Shift+P, type "grid", press Enter. The checkerboard behind the
picture appears; do it again and it goes.
