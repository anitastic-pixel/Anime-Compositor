# D-380 proposal: Channel Blur in After Effects' Blurriness scale

**Status: PROPOSED on 2026-10-09. Nothing is built or changed. The choice is the owner's.**

**Decided on 2026-10-09: the owner chose (c) ("c"). Built as B-259; see `verification/B-259_playtest.md`.**

## What you will see

Picture: `verification/D-380 pictures/channel_blur_scale.png`. Three rows: Blurriness 10, Blurriness 30 (all channels), and red only at 20. Columns: the original, Channel Blur today, Channel Blur as proposed, and our Gaussian Blur at the same number.

- **Today**, Channel Blur at 10 already smears the stripes, checker and text away completely, and at 30 the picture is a grey-brown fog.
- **Proposed**, Channel Blur at 10 looks the same as our Gaussian Blur at 10: soft, but you can still see the stripes' block, the checker and the coloured lines. At 30 it again matches Gaussian Blur at 30.
- **Red only at 20**: today the red spreads into a wide red haze over the whole frame. Proposed, the red fringe stays close to the red things (the red bar, the orange oval, the red line), which looks more like the colour-fringe trick people use Channel Blur for.

## The evidence, and how strong it is

**Weak. Nothing we found says what scale After Effects' Channel Blur uses.**

- Skia's Skottie (it plays After Effects files) lists the effects it supports in `modules/skottie/src/effects/Effects.cpp`. Gaussian Blur is there, Channel Blur and Fast Box Blur are not. Its Gaussian Blur and Drop Shadow both turn the number into a spread with 0.3, the constant D-321 uses.
- lottie-web (it plays After Effects files too) has only Gaussian Blur and Drop Shadow among the blurs. Its Gaussian Blur uses 0.3, but its Drop Shadow divides softness by 4 (0.25). So the two players do not even agree on one constant for every blur, and neither has Channel Blur.
- Adobe's own effect page (helpx.adobe.com) returned "forbidden" and the archived copy could not be reached, so Adobe's wording was not read this time.
- An Adobe community thread (Premiere Pro, "Desenfoque de canal", 2025) says Channel Blur was removed from Premiere and suggests rebuilding it with Gaussian Blur on separate channels. No one there says the numbers match.
- No forum comparison of Channel Blur against Gaussian Blur at the same number was found.

So the case for 0.3 rests on a guess: After Effects' built-in blurs probably share Gaussian Blur's Blurriness scale, and Adobe treats Gaussian Blur as Channel Blur's stand-in. It is likely, not proven. You have no After Effects to measure it, so it cannot be settled here.

## The options

- **(a) Switch Channel Blur to 0.3 x Blurriness for everyone.** Every saved project with Channel Blur would blur about three times less when opened. D-321 did **not** do this to Gaussian Blur: it kept old files exactly as they were (see (c)). Taking (a) would therefore be a stricter change than D-321, accepted as a fix with no conversion. FX-CHBLUR-001 to 014's expected numbers would have to be regenerated, which is a fixture change and your call.
- **(b) Keep sigma = Blurriness** and keep the known difference written in EFFECTS.md as it is now. Nothing changes.
- **(c) Do what D-321 did for Gaussian Blur: a Units choice in Effect controls.** "Blurriness (After Effects)" means spread = 0.3 x the number, faded out over 6.5 spreads, exactly D-321's rule; "Sigma (older projects)" is today's rule. A Channel Blur added from now on is in Blurriness; a saved file without units stays Sigma and draws exactly as before. FX-CHBLUR-001 to 014 stay as they are (they have no units, so they mean Sigma); new fixtures would be written for the Blurriness cases before the build, from `tools/channel_blur_reference.py`'s rule with D-321's kernel.

## Recommendation

**(c)**, because it follows the path you already accepted in D-321, makes Channel Blur and Gaussian Blur agree on the same number, and moves no old picture and no existing fixture, so if the guess is ever shown wrong only new projects are affected.

## What would be re-recorded if (a) were chosen

- `Fixtures/channel_blur/expected_channel_blur.json` (FX-CHBLUR-001 to 010 change; 011 to 014 are invalid-value cases and stay), regenerated by `tools/channel_blur_reference.py` changed to 0.3 x Blurriness.
- The card's Channel Blur pictures in `verification/B-223_gpu_blurs_table.md` (164 checks) and `verification/D-363_channel_blur_unsharp_table.md`.
- Any pinned picture or tutorial project that uses Channel Blur would come out sharper, with about a third of today's blur, and its checks would be re-pinned.

Under (c) nothing is re-recorded; only new fixtures and a new playtest are added.
