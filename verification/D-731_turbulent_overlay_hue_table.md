# D-731: Turbulent Noise, Overlay and Hue

B-601: `core.turbulent_noise` takes Overlay and Hue, by the owner's specification decision of 2026-10-13, which regenerated FX-TURBNOISE-032 (Overlay) and FX-TURBNOISE-060 (Hue) from refused to drawn. Overlay is the Timeline's Overlay formula (D-301, `grade::mixer`: 2 b g where the layer b is up to one half, else 1 - 2 (1 - b)(1 - g)), taken on the straight linear colour as every other mode of this effect is; the Timeline takes it on the encoded colour. Hue is the W3C Compositing and Blending Level 1 Hue, SetLum(SetSat(noise, Sat(layer)), Lum(layer)), the same basis as D-730's Saturation, Color and Luminosity, not a match to After Effects. The noise is a grey, so Hue gives the layer its own grey at its own lightness, the same picture as Saturation and Color. Each mixes by Opacity as the other modes do. Every expected pixel is `Fixtures/turbulent_noise/expected_turbulent_noise.json` (032) and `expected_turbulent_more.json` (060), regenerated by `tools/turbulent_noise_reference.py` and `tools/turbulent_more_reference.py` in their own commit before this code. Tolerance 2e-5.
