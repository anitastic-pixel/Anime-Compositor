# B-24h: a point added to a path, from the window

Generated by `app/src/main.rs`, module `editing`. Re-run with `cargo test --workspace`.

B-24d wrote down a limit: a point could not be added to or taken off a path that had keys, because a path's points are the path's and every key holds them all. D-79 lifts it by doing both at once - the point is cut into the base and into every key at the same place along the same segment - and the page reaches it with the pen over the path to add one, the pen over a point to take it off, and Delete to take off every point chosen at once (D-80, B-24j: Alt and a click converts a point now, as it does in After Effects, so the pen carries adding and taking off, where After Effects keeps them).

Every row calls what the window calls, on `Fixtures/projects/cel_holds_project.json`, and reads back what the page is given: how many points the base and each key hold, and the outline at a frame off the same answer the boxes come from, which is what the renderer actually drew.

## What this does not cover

Whether the pen lands on the segment a person meant, and whether the shape looks unmoved on the picture afterwards. That is `verification/B-24h_path_point_playtest.md`, for a person. That the curve itself does not move, to fourteen decimal places, and that the drawn pixels move by no more than flattening, is the core's, in `verification/B-24h_path_point_table.md`.
