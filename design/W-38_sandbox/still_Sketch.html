The idea
Sketching left the viewer and has its own workspace. The panels and the timeline are gone: tools on the left, the page in the middle, layers on the right, frames along the bottom.
What the numbers point at
1
Tools
Brush, pencil, eraser; three sizes; five colours; undo.
2
The page
The cut's frame shows faintly underneath. Turn it like paper with the buttons above it.
3
Layers
Show or hide each, pick the one you draw on, add more. A layer draws on one frame, or on the whole cut (for notes that stay).
4
Frames
Step through the cut. ● marks a frame with its own sketch. Onion skin shows the sketched frames either side in red and green.
5
Where sketches show
Optionally over the Compose viewer too. Never exported.
Try it
Draw on the picture. Pick Pose fix and draw again: it is a separate layer you can hide. Step to frame 108 and turn on Onion skin to see frame 107's sketch faintly.
Trade-off. To sketch you switch workspace, and to see the result you switch back, unless "Also over the Compose viewer" is ticked.
