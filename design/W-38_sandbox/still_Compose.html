Sandbox · Compose workspace
One screen, everything docked
Whole app AWhole app B (workspaces)Top bar ASearch BEffect controls BViewer ATimeline A + BProject A–DEffects C
The idea
Whole app A's single docked screen, close to After Effects, with the film icon top left. Whole app B's workspaces are the tabs at the top right. No effects list is docked: Ctrl+Space opens the finder in the middle, so the viewer gets the room.
What the numbers point at
1
Top bar A, items 1 to 5, in one row
Menus; the cut with ‹ ›; then three small icons: its status, the health warning (click it) and the running export (click it to watch). Save shows an orange dot until saved. Hover any icon for its name.
2
Search B, item 8 of Top bar A
One box for commands, menus, layers and help, with scope chips. When your letters also match effects, its last line hands you to the finder.
3
Effects, Ctrl+Space
Opens the effects finder in the middle of the screen.
4
Workspaces
Compose, Animate, Sketch and Render. Each keeps its own layout. Frame, keys and sketches are shared.
5
Effect controls B
Short rows in one tree, the blue switch, a slider on the chosen row only, ◀ ◆ ▶ on demand. Drag a blue number.
6
Viewer A, in one row
Play, quality, Show, Compare, Size, Paper and the overlays as small icons; hover for the name. The slate sits just above the picture, never on it.
7
Timeline, Balanced look
A tab per composition. Timeline, Both or Graph sit beside the frame number; the Sheet button is at the right, by "Cut 012 · 8 layers". Switches, Mode and Parent columns, a green sound wave. Double-click Precomp 1 for the map.
8
Notes on the ruler
Like Balanced’s "Key pose". The Note button adds one at the playhead, or double-click the ruler. Click a note to rename or delete it. Notes also show in the sheet’s Action column.
9
Ready frames
The green line under the work area marks frames already in memory: they play at full speed. Play or step and it grows; an edit clears the frames it changes. The count beside the timecode says how many, and hovering it says how much memory.
Try it
Double-click the ruler near frame 110, type "hair settles" and press Enter. Drag along the ruler. Twirl Hair (▸). Click Both, then drag a diamond in the graph: the hair moves in the viewer and the number in Effect controls follows. Type "gl" in the search box.
