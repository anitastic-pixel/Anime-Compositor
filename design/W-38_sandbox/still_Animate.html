Sandbox · Animate workspace
Rows, graph and sheet together
Timeline ATimeline BEffect controls BViewer AWhole app B (workspaces)
The idea
Timeline A and B combined. The rows stay on top, the graph sits right under them on the same frames, and one red playhead runs through both. The sheet stands on the side and hides with one button.
What the numbers point at
1
Timeline, Both, Graph
Beside the frame number. Both is the default here. Each workspace remembers its own choice.
2
Hair, twirled open
Position, Rotation and Glow › Radius, with ◀ ◆ ▶ to step and set keys.
3
The graph
Tick which settings to show. Drag a diamond: up and down changes the value, sideways moves the key. Easy ease, Linear and Hold change the curve.
4
The sheet
Frame by frame: Hair, Body, BG, Action and Dialogue. The lit row is the playhead; click a row to go there. Body 42 is struck out: it is missing. The Sheet button at the right of the timeline shows or hides it.
5
A smaller viewer
Only the play row, so timing has the room.
6
Effect controls
The same settings, so you can type an exact value.
Try it
Drag the pink Radius diamond at frame 118 up: the glow grows in the viewer. Click Linear, then Hold, and watch the curve change shape. Press the Sheet button to hide the sheet.
Trade-off. Rows and graph share the height, so each gets about half. Graph alone gives the curves the whole panel.
My reading. I put the sheet as a full-height column on the right so its 48 frames fit without scrolling. It is on in Animate and off in Compose.
